Authoring checks
What happens when this enters the game?

Exported YAML is an authored definition, not an instruction that directly changes the game. BlueHarvest must load it, bind it to a readable object, actor, terminal, or event, and check discovery/knowledge through the simulation.

Dialogue choices and revealed knowledge are only descriptive here. At runtime, the simulation decides whether an interaction occurred and records facts through trusted C# handlers.

IDs, facts, facility codes and references are checked against the content library's own rules, so a pack that exports cleanly also passes content validate's ID checks. Item and device references are written with their pack (core:DEV-ADM-001), and that pack is listed as a dependency.
